Profile

Dark. Structured. Always intentional.
A wardrobe built around silhouette, edge, and the discipline to never dress average.

Style Signature

Edge over everything.
Structure as language.

The wardrobe skews dark and minimal, built on avant-garde Japanese construction and sharp ORTTU tailoring. Drama and edge lead — formality and structure support without ever softening the intention.

Skin exposure is deliberately low. The power here is in silhouette, not surface. Pieces earn their place through what they do to a room, not just how they photograph.
